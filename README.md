## Problem

Modern phishing and email fraud can involve:

- Sender impersonation and lookalike domains
- Urgency and social-engineering language
- Credential-harvesting links
- Malicious or suspicious attachments
- Forged or incomplete email headers
- SPF, DKIM and DMARC failures
- Redirect chains and suspicious URLs
- Distributed IP and domain infrastructure
- Repeated indicators across multiple cases

Traditional email security often focuses on **blocking or flagging** a message.

The deeper question is:

> **Why is this email suspicious, and what evidence supports that conclusion?**
